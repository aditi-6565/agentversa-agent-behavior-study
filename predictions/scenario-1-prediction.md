## Prediction

When specific case information becomes available in Scenario 1, I predict that Raksha will continue to prioritize the safety, privacy, and dignity of victims and witnesses.

I predict that Raksha will first assess the available information and identify any potential safety, privacy, or participation risks before taking substantive action.

I also predict that she will seek relevant information or coordinate with the appropriate justice-system agents when a decision involves risks or responsibilities outside her authority.

---

## Expected Decision-Making Behavior

Based on the Version 1 design and Scenario 0 baseline, I expect Raksha to:

- Focus on the safety and privacy of victims and witnesses.
- Avoid making unsupported legal conclusions.
- Distinguish known information from assumptions.
- Request or consider additional information when the available information is insufficient.
- Coordinate with other agents rather than acting outside her authority.
- Consider the rights of the accused when making recommendations.
- Prefer protective and reversible measures when uncertainty is high.
- Escalate issues requiring human judgment when they exceed her authority.

---

## Expected Response to Conflicting Interests

If Scenario 1 introduces competing interests, I predict that Raksha will attempt to balance:

- Victim and witness safety
- Privacy and confidentiality
- Autonomy and informed consent
- Procedural requirements
- Rights of the accused
- The need for accurate and sufficient information

If safety and disclosure obligations conflict, I predict that Raksha will document the competing concerns and seek appropriate coordination or escalation rather than resolving the conflict independently.

---

## Expected Coordination

I predict that Raksha will become more active in coordinating with other agents once specific case information is available.

The likely purpose of coordination will be to:

- Obtain relevant information.
- Clarify responsibilities.
- Address safety or privacy concerns.
- Identify appropriate protective measures.
- Escalate matters that require human review.

## Expected Risk Posture

**Predicted risk posture:** Cautious

I expect Raksha to be more cautious when:

- Information is incomplete.
- A victim or witness may face harm or intimidation.

- The decision exceeds her defined authority.
